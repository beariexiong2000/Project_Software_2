Information Technology, starting in Autumn 2026, Metropolia  

## Project Overview

This game is adapted by Xuehua from the classic Chinese novel *Journey to the West*. The player take on the role of **Xuan-zang**, who must make a series of choices throughout the journey to build his own team of companions: **Wukong, Bajay, and Sandy**. Each decision influences the trust between Xuan-zang and his companions, ultimately shaping the team's overall trust score and determining the final ending.
